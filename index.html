Who is behind it?

Meet Florencia.

The tour was created by Florencia Migliorisi, a former bartender who has also worked with beverage companies and has dedicated more than 20 years to hospitality.

Florencia studied Political Science, loves yoga and has always had a passion for bringing people together. For her, going out isn't just about finding a good bar. It's about the people you meet, the stories you create and the memories you take home.

Some of the funniest things that have happened on our tours? People have fallen in love. Friendships have started. Complete strangers have ended up dancing together. That's the kind of night we're trying to create.

FAQ

Good to know.

How long is the tour?

3.5 hours, with three bars and a host with you the whole time.

Is it only for travelers?

Travelers from all over the world are our main guests, but locals looking for a great night out are welcome too.

When does it run?

Every night, all year round. Friday and Saturday fill up quickly.

Do I need to bring money?

Drinks and shots are part of the experience. Payment and extras are handled through the booking platform you choose.

Will I get photos?

Yes. We shoot on analog film throughout the night and send you a digital folder with your photos afterwards.

Where do I book?

The fastest way is WhatsApp to check availability. You can also book on Airbnb or Viator. Use the buttons in the booking section above.

Can we keep going after the tour?

Yes. If you're still up for it, our hosts will help you continue the night.
